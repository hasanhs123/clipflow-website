Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipFlow handles information when a user uses our video publishing service.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features a user uses, ClipFlow may process:

  • Account and profile information made available through TikTok's authorization flow, such as a TikTok user identifier and basic profile information when required for the connected service.
  • Authorization information, such as access and refresh tokens, needed to keep an authorized connection working.
  • Video files or media URLs that a user submits for publishing.
  • Publishing status information, such as a post identifier or processing status.
  • Technical information needed to secure and operate the service, such as IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, and error logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate a connected TikTok account, submit authorized publishing requests, display publishing status, provide support, maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the reliability of the service.

3. TikTok data

When a user connects TikTok, authorization is handled through TikTok's authentication flow. ClipFlow does not request or store the user's TikTok password. TikTok data is used only for the purposes disclosed in the ClipFlow application and this policy.

4. Sharing

We may use service providers that help us host, secure, monitor, or operate ClipFlow. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the service, users, or others.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining an authorized connection, providing support, meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. User choices and deletion requests

Users may request deletion of information associated with their ClipFlow account or disconnect their TikTok authorization. To make a request, contact us using the details on the Contact page. We may need to verify the request before completing it.

8. Children's privacy

ClipFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
